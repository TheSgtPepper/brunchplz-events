What happens

About 90 minutes of Christmas magic, in pajamas.

  1. Check in at the station. We check your tickets at the door, and the elves are waiting.
  2. Join the elves’ mission. Santa’s trolley lost power, and the elves need the kids’ help to get it running again.
  3. Cocoa, cookies, letters to Santa, and a full-on PJ dance party.
  4. Fill the Spirit-O-Meter. Every bit of Christmas spirit lights more bulbs, until Santa’s trolley gets its power back.
  5. Santa arrives. He calls every kid’s name off his scroll, sits down to chat with each one, and poses for your family photo.

FAQ

How much are tickets?

$25 per person, under 2 free on a lap. Grown-ups need a ticket too.

What’s included?

Hot cocoa and a cookie, a sleigh bell for every child, and a professional family photo with Santa.

How long is it?

90 minutes: 3:00–4:30 PM or 5:00–6:30 PM, every Sunday from Nov 29 to Dec 20.

Is there a train ride?

No, it’s not a train ride. The mission happens right here at Passenger & Rail Co.

What should we wear?

Pajamas are the vibe, but not required. Please, not the kind with the button-up booty flap. I’m looking at you.

How will Santa know my kid’s name?

Checkout asks a few optional questions for each ticket: your child’s first name, something they’re proud of this year, and what they’re hoping for. Answers stay private, on Santa’s cheat sheet only.

Where is it?

Passenger & Rail Co., at the rear of Bucci’s Italian Scratch Kitchen in Marion, Ohio.

Are tickets refundable?

All sales are final, but you can transfer your tickets to a friend. Just have them email hello@brunchplz.com with their children’s info.
